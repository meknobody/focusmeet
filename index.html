Privacy Policy

FocusMeet Student Transparency & Compliance Hub

🛡️ Student-First Privacy Commitment

FocusMeet operates on a strict Zero Server-Side Tracking architecture. Your tasks, timetable schedule, study streaks, and Pomodoro statistics never leave your device.

1. Data Storage & Ownership

FocusMeet stores all user account data, study minutes, custom tasks, syllabus progress, and theme preferences entirely in your web browser's client-side storage (HTML5 localStorage). We do not operate remote databases or user tracking servers that profile your study habits.

2. Zero Third-Party Advertising & Keystroke Tracking

We do not partner with ad brokers, data aggregators, or behavioral marketing networks. FocusMeet never sells, rents, or monetizes user information. There are no background keyloggers, screen scrapers, or surveillance scripts running inside this workspace.

3. Third-Party CDN Services

To deliver high performance and beautiful visuals without hosting overhead, FocusMeet loads client-side runtime libraries:

  • Tailwind CSS CDN: Delivering modern utility styling.
  • Google Fonts: Serving the Inter and JetBrains Mono typefaces.
  • Spline 3D Runtime: Powering the interactive 3D study desk.
  • GitHub Pages: Providing secure static SSL hosting.
4. Data Deletion & Control

You have unconditional control over your data. You can completely erase your data at any moment by clearing your browser cache or resetting local storage for this domain.

5. Student & Child Privacy

FocusMeet is designed to be fully compliant with educational privacy expectations (COPPA and FERPA principles) because zero personally identifiable information is transmitted or stored on remote servers.

🍪 Local Storage & Cookie Disclosure

FocusMeet uses zero tracking cookies. Instead, it utilizes your browser's standard HTML5 Local Storage solely for offline functionality.

1. Storage Keys Utilized
Key Name Purpose Duration
focusmeet_active_session_v3 Stores currently active user session Persistent until Logout
focusmeet_registered_users_v2 Stores registered accounts on this device Persistent
focusmeet_student_state_v4 Stores tasks, schedule, study minutes, & XP Persistent
focusmeet_theme Remembers selected UI theme (Indigo, Dark, etc.) Persistent
focusmeet_cookie_consent Records acknowledgment of this notice Persistent
2. Managing Local Storage

You can inspect, export, or clear these keys at any time via your browser's Developer Tools (Application → Local Storage) or through your browser's clear browsing history settings.
